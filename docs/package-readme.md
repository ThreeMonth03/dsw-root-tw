# Taiwan DSW Knowledge Model

This knowledge model adapts the Common DSW Knowledge Model for research data
management in Taiwan.

## Status

Version 0.1.0 is a meeting draft. Its Taiwan legal wording has authoritative
sources and engineering review, but has not yet received final legal or domain
approval. It is not legal advice.

## Parent

Forked from `dsw:root:2.7.0`.

## Draft scope

- Taiwan personal-data protection and cross-border review
- human-subject research, consent, reuse, and ethics review
- human biobanks and research involving Indigenous Peoples
- medical human trials, scientific use of animals, infectious biological
  materials, wildlife, and other physical samples
- artificial intelligence, National Health Insurance data, national core
  critical technologies, related trade secrets, export controls, patents, and
  public-sector records
- security, retention, intellectual property, confidentiality, and data-use
  restrictions

The inherited Common DSW history remains intact. This draft changes the English
title and guidance of 45 high-risk questions, replaces 29 inherited answer,
choice, and reference texts that otherwise retain EU-specific assumptions, and
adds two per-project Taiwan legal-routing questions with 11 screening choices.
The new entity UUIDs remain stable across later package versions.
